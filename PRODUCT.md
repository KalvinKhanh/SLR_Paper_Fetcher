# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers conducting systematic literature reviews who collect research papers and inspect claims, evidence, warrants, and their relationships.

## Product Purpose

The application gathers authorized research PDFs and RIS citations from DOI-based inputs, then helps reviewers inspect source-grounded paper analyses and argument graphs. Success means reviewers can quickly understand how a claim is supported, trace propositions to their sources, and distinguish AI-generated suggestions from accepted review decisions.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, and academic. The interface should help users examine evidence carefully without implying that a structurally valid argument is scientifically correct.

## Anti-references

- Pale or indistinguishable role colors that make Claim, Grounds, Warrant, Backing, Qualifier, and Rebuttal hard to tell apart.
- Interfaces that depend on color alone to communicate argumentative roles or review states.

## Design Principles

- Make argumentative roles and relationship direction apparent at a glance.
- Keep propositions traceable to source text and paper context.
- Separate model suggestions from reviewer decisions.
- Represent missing or uncertain argument components honestly.
- Preserve the distinction between graph structure and scientific validity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable role labels in addition to color, provide keyboard access to graph controls and nodes, and respect reduced-motion preferences. No formal WCAG conformance level has been specified.
